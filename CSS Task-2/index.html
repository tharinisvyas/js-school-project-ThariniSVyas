Getting into My Dream College (July 2023)

The day I received my offer from Manipal Institute of Technology, Bengaluru was unforgettable. It felt like years of effort, ambition, and late-night prayers had finally paid off. I remember my heart racing as I read the email — I was officially going to my dream college. That moment sparked a wave of excitement, gratitude, and belief that anything is possible.

From Family Bonds to Frozen Frontiers (April 2025)

Our family trip to Sikkim took a powerful turn as we reached Nathula Pass and Baba Harbhajan Singh Mandir. Hearing the story of the brave soldier who continues to guard the borders even after death gave me goosebumps. Surrounded by snow and stillness, I felt a strange mix of pride, calm, and awe — something I’ll never forget.
